About Recitte

Small, consistent actions. Real transformation.

Recitte is a purpose-driven initiative reconnecting Muslims with the Qur'an in a consistent and meaningful way. We imagine a world where a reasonable number of Muslims give just a few minutes each day to the Book of Allah.

Guided by the Qur'an and the Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ, we build practical tools — reminders, recitation aids, learning resources and reflective prompts — that make even the smallest moments of connection possible.

About us · Who we are

A brand committed to a lifelong relationship with the Qur'an.

Recitte is rooted in a simple idea: reconnecting Muslims with the Qur'an in a consistent and meaningful way. Not only recitation — but understanding, reflection, implementation, and ultimately, seeking the pleasure of Allah.

Vision

A world where a reasonable number of Muslims dedicate a few minutes each day to engaging with the Qur'an.

Mission

To provide practical, accessible tools that help Muslims build the Qur'an into their daily routines.
